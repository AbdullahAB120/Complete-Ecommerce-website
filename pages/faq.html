FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions.

How long does shipping usually take?

Our standard shipping time is between 3–7 business days depending on your delivery location and product availability. Orders placed during weekends or public holidays may require additional processing time. Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a confirmation email or SMS with a tracking number so you can monitor the delivery status. During peak shopping seasons, delivery may take slightly longer, but our team always works to ensure your order reaches you as quickly as possible.

Can I return or exchange a product?

Yes, we offer a 7-day return and exchange policy for eligible items. Products must be returned in their original condition, unused, and with all tags and packaging intact. If the item is damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, we will arrange a replacement or refund after verification. Please note that certain products, such as personal care items or customized products, may not be eligible for return due to hygiene or personalization reasons.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We support a wide range of secure payment options, including Visa, MasterCard, American Express, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and Cash on Delivery in selected areas. All online payments are processed through secure payment gateways with encryption technology to protect your financial information. Customers can choose the most convenient payment method during checkout without any hidden charges.

How can I track my order?

After your order has been confirmed and dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email or SMS. Simply visit the Order Tracking page on our website, enter your order number or tracking ID, and you will see the latest updates regarding your shipment. If you experience any issues with tracking, our customer support team is available to assist you promptly.

What should I do if I receive a damaged or incorrect item?

We sincerely apologize if you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product. Please contact our customer support team within 48 hours of delivery and provide clear photos of the product and packaging. Once our team verifies the issue, we will arrange a replacement, exchange, or refund at no additional cost. Our goal is to ensure every customer receives exactly what they ordered.

Can I cancel my order after placing it?

Yes, you can cancel your order before it has been shipped. Simply log into your account, visit the "My Orders" section, and select the cancellation option if it is still available. If your order has already been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible, but you can request a return after receiving the package, subject to our return policy.

Do I need to create an account to place an order?

No, you may place an order as a guest customer without creating an account. However, registering for an account provides several benefits, including faster checkout, easy order tracking, saved delivery addresses, access to exclusive offers, and a complete purchase history. Creating an account only takes a few moments and helps make future shopping much more convenient.

Are my personal and payment details secure?

Absolutely. We use advanced SSL encryption and trusted payment gateways to protect all customer information. Your payment details are never stored on our servers, and we follow strict security practices to keep your personal data safe. We are committed to maintaining customer privacy and ensuring a secure shopping experience at every step.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to selected international destinations. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on the destination country, customs regulations, and courier services. International customers are responsible for any applicable customs duties or import taxes imposed by their local authorities. You can check shipping availability during the checkout process before completing your purchase.

How can I apply a discount or promo code?

If you have a valid promo or discount code, simply enter it in the Promo Code field during checkout before completing your payment. Once applied successfully, the discount will be reflected in your order total instantly. Please note that some promotional codes may have expiration dates, minimum purchase requirements, or restrictions on specific products.

What happens if my delivery is delayed?

Although we strive to deliver every order on time, unexpected circumstances such as severe weather, public holidays, courier delays, or high order volumes may occasionally affect delivery schedules. If your order is delayed, you can check its latest status using the tracking page or contact our customer support team for assistance. We will do our best to resolve any delivery issues as quickly as possible.

How can I contact customer support?

Our customer support team is available to assist you with any questions regarding orders, returns, payments, or product information. You can reach us through live chat, email, phone, or our contact form during business hours. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours and provide fast, friendly, and reliable support to ensure you have the best possible shopping experience.

The customer is at the heart of our unique business model, which includes design.
